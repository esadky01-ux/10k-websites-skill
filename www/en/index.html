Frequently asked questions

How is the price calculated?

A base fare of €10 plus €1.99 per kilometre driven. The price shown at booking is final: no night or weekend surcharges.

How does payment work?

You pay a 20% deposit online via Bancontact, credit card or Apple Pay (secured by Mollie). The rest is paid to the driver, in cash or by card.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancel more than 24 hours before departure and the deposit is fully refunded. Within 24 hours of departure, the deposit is non-refundable.

What if my flight is delayed?

At airports, waiting is free for 60 minutes after landing. At other addresses, the first 15 minutes are free, then 0.50 € per minute.

Do you drive abroad?

Yes: Germany, France, Luxembourg and the Netherlands. Paris, Amsterdam, Cologne or Schiphol, the price is calculated the same way.

How many passengers and bags fit?

Up to 7 passengers and 6 large suitcases in our Mercedes Vito. For 4 passengers or fewer, the Tesla Model 3 or the station wagon is plenty.

How much is a taxi from Brussels to Brussels Airport (Zaventem, BRU)?

From central Brussels to Brussels Airport (Zaventem), the airport transfer is about 13 km, so €35.87 at the per-kilometre rate (€10 + €1.99 per km). From Ghent (about 56 km) it is €121.44. The price confirmed at booking is final; for the fixed airport price, ask for today's price on WhatsApp. Pay a 20% deposit online and the rest to the driver.

How much is a taxi from Brussels to Charleroi Airport (CRL)?

From Brussels to Charleroi Airport it is about 55 km, so €119.45 at the per-kilometre rate (€10 + €1.99 per km). The price confirmed at booking is final. At the airport, waiting is free for 60 minutes after landing.

Do you drive at night and early in the morning?

Yes, we are available every day, 24 hours a day, including for early flights and late arrivals. There is no night or weekend surcharge: the price confirmed at booking does not change.

How much is a cross-border taxi from Brussels to Paris or Amsterdam?

We drive to Germany, France, Luxembourg and the Netherlands with the same formula (€10 + €1.99 per km). From Brussels: Amsterdam Schiphol is about 193 km, so €394.07; Paris is about 315 km, so €636.85. The exact price is shown before you book.

Privacy policy

We only collect the data needed for your booking: name, email, phone, pickup and drop-off address, date and time of the ride.

This data is used to provide your ride, process your payment and send your confirmation. It is shared only with our service providers: Mollie (payment), Google (route calculation), Brevo (emails) and your driver.

Billing data is kept for 7 years, as required by Belgian accounting law. All other data is deleted no later than 12 months after the ride.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing Europataxisrl@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
